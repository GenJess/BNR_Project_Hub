Competitor Landscape

Who You're Up Against

Understanding the competitive field shapes where to focus first.

National Competitors

Platforms like Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor dominate broad terms via domain authority. Difficult to outrank organically — focus on local map pack instead.

Local Map Pack Rivals

Local Houston operators in the Google 3-pack win via GBP optimization + volume of 5-star reviews. Most lack blog content — a gap B&R can exploit.

Key Gaps to Exploit

Most local competitors have no blog, no video testimonials, and no structured data markup. A content strategy targeting seasonal and service-area keywords would give B&R a clear edge within 3–6 months.

Action Plan

Prioritized Recommendations

Sequenced by impact and speed to results. Start at the top.

  1. Optimize Google Business Profile — Week 1
    Complete all GBP fields: service areas (Houston, Conroe, The Woodlands, Katy), hours, photos (before/after jobs), service categories. Post weekly updates. This is the single highest-impact action for local map pack visibility.
  2. Fix On-Page SEO Fundamentals — Week 1–2
    Add keyword-rich title tags and meta descriptions to every page. Implement proper H1→H2→H3 hierarchy. Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema markup. Target primary keyword in the H1 of each service page.
  3. Launch a Review Generation System — Week 2
    Send every completed job a direct link to leave a Google review. Even 10–15 new 5-star reviews can push a GBP listing into the local 3-pack. This is the fastest ROI action available.
  4. Build Local Directory Backlinks — Week 3–4
    Submit consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) to: Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Yelp, BBB, Houzz, NextDoor Business, and local Houston Chamber directories. This builds domain authority and citation trust.
  5. Create Seasonal Blog Content — Month 2+
    Publish 2 articles/month targeting local long-tail keywords: "Power washing before hurricane season Houston", "Spring cleaning pressure wash checklist", "How often should you soft wash a roof in Texas". This compounds over time.
  6. Launch the Redesigned Website — Month 2–3
    The Vercel proposal app (bnr-power-washing.vercel.app) is the foundation. Launch with: online quote form, embedded Google reviews, service area map (Mapbox), clear CTAs above the fold on every page.
